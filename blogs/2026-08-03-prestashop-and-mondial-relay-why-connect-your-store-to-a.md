Connecting PrestaShop to Mondial Relay allows you to automate shipments, generate shipping labels, and track packages more easily. However, this integration is no longer sufficient when shipment volumes increase, multiple carriers are used, or manual processing leads to errors. This article explains where the limitations lie and why an OMS/TMS architecture offers greater scalability.
