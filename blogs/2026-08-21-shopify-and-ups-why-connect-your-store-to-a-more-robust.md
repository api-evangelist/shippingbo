Connecting Shopify to UPS makes it easier to automate shipments, generate shipping labels, and track packages. But this integration is no longer sufficient once volumes increase, multiple carriers are used, or manual processing leads to errors. This article explains where the limits lie and why an OMS/TMS architecture becomes more scalable.
