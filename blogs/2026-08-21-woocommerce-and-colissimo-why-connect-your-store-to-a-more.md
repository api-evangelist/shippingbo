Connecting WooCommerce to Colissimo makes it easier to automate shipping, generate shipping labels, and track shipments. However, this integration is no longer sufficient once volumes increase, multiple carriers are used, or manual processing leads to errors. This article explains where the limitations lie and why an OMS/TMS architecture becomes more scalable.
