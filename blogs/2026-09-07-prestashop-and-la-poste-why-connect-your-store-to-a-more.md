Connecting PrestaShop to La Poste makes it easier to automate shipments, generate shipping labels, and track packages. However, this integration is no longer sufficient once volumes increase, multiple carriers are used, or manual processing leads to errors. This article explains where the limitations lie and why an OMS/TMS architecture becomes more scalable.
