Ship From Store allows a retailer to use its stores as fulfillment and shipping centers for e-commerce orders. This model relies on reliable inventory management, precise allocation rules, and coordination among stores, orders, and carriers. Learn how it works, its benefits, its limitations, and the prerequisites that must be met before implementing it.
