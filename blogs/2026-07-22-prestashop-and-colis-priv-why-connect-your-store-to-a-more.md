Connecting Prestashop Colis Privé makes it easier to automate shipments, generate labels and track. But this connection is no longer sufficient when volumes increase, multiple carriers are used or manual processing creates errors. This article explains where the limit lies and why an OMS / TMS architecture becomes more scalable.
